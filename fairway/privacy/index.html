Fairway Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026 · Provided by 株式会社MKSP

日本語はこちら

Fairway is a golf practice app. You rate your swings at the driving range and see your progress. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. The short version: Fairway creates an account for you automatically, without asking for your name or email, so your practice is backed up and you can join your area's team; Sign in with Apple is optional and keeps your account if you change phones. We collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app, and we never sell your data or show ads.

1. Data that stays on your device

Your practice log is kept on your iPhone: sessions, swing ratings, clubs, direction, curve and contact tags, focus notes and settings. It is also synced to your account on our server, as described in section 2. It is included in your device's own iCloud or computer backups if you have those turned on, which Apple manages under your Apple account. Deleting the app deletes this data from the device; delete your account (section 2) to remove it from our server.

Fairway also has a web version (app.fairway.tokyo). There, your practice log is stored in that browser on that device, and clearing the browser's site data deletes it there. When you first save a practice on the web, an account is created for it automatically, as in the app (section 2), and it is synced the same way. The web version sends the same kind of analytics described below; its random identifier is kept in that browser until you clear the site data.

In US states whose laws require it, the app asks Apple for your age range (for example "18 or over"). Apple shows its own prompt, and the answer is kept only on your device.

Fairway does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location unless you choose to sign in with Apple (section 2), to use your location when picking your area, or to add a range photo to a post (section 3).

2. Your account (created automatically; Sign in with Apple or Google optional)

When you first open Fairway, the app creates an account for you automatically. It contains no name, email or Apple ID: just a random identifier, a random nickname (for example "Weekend Golfer 482") and a random golfer character, which you can change any time in Settings → Edit profile. If you later choose to sign in with Apple, Apple is linked to this same account, so everything stays. On the web version you can also sign in with Google.

What we receive from Apple (only if you sign in with Apple): an Apple user ID (a random identifier that is specific to Fairway), your email address (or an Apple private relay address if you choose to hide your email) and your name, if you share it. You can also set a nickname and pick a golfer character in the app, which we store too.

What we receive from Google (web version only, only if you sign in with Google): a Google user ID, your email address, your name and a link to your Google profile picture. We use them only for your account (the name as a nickname suggestion); we don't use the picture.

What we sync: your practice log (sessions, swings, focus notes and tags), the round scores you enter (date, score, holes and course name), your badges, and a few settings (your bag, club lofts, language, pledge, weekly goal, nickname and golfer). We do this so your history is backed up, counts for your team in the Clubhouse, and is the same on every device where you sign in with Apple. Other settings, such as appearance, stay on the device.

Where it is stored: with our service provider Supabase Inc., in Tokyo, Japan (AWS ap-northeast-1). Supabase processes the data only on our behalf.

How long we keep it: until you delete your account. An automatic account that has no Apple sign-in and hasn't been used for 30 days is deleted with everything in it (the practice log on your phone stays; the app makes a new account the next time it's needed). In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This removes your account and synced data from our servers, and we attempt to revoke Fairway's access to your Apple account. If that doesn't go through, you can remove Fairway yourself: on your iPhone, go to Settings → [your name] → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple, choose Fairway and tap Stop Using Apple ID. Copies in database backups are removed as the backups roll off, within 7 days.

Emails: we do not send you any emails. Your account stores a marketing-email preference, which is off by default; we will only turn it on if you separately agree to it in the future.

The analytics and crash reporting described in section 4 are not linked to your account.

3. Clubhouse

The Clubhouse lets you practise with friends in a small group (a "crew") and play for your area's team on a local board. Everyone with a Fairway account can see the board; once you pick the area you practise in (the app asks after a practice, or any time from the Clubhouse tab), you're on your area's team. There's no separate way to leave it: deleting your account removes you from it (see "Deleting" below).

What we store when you join: the area you practise in (for example a Tokyo ward, a Japanese prefecture, a US state or a country; never an address or coordinates), your golfer character (the one in your profile; the app picks one at random until you choose), your time zone, your crew, the cheers and nudges you send and receive, the people you block, reports you make, and a push-notification token for your phone.

Location: when you join, the app can suggest your area from your current location. Only if you tap to use your location does it ask for permission (approximate location by default), read your location once and turn it into an area; your iPhone uses Apple's location service to look up the place name. Your coordinates are not sent to us, and only the area you confirm is saved. Fairway does not use motion data.

What others see: people in your crew see your name, golfer, and how many range days and balls you logged each day of the current week, plus cheers. On the local board, your range days and balls count toward your area's total for everyone, and your name, golfer, area, range days and balls can appear in its list of top golfers for other Clubhouse members on the same board (people you block never see you there). In the Clubhouse feed, every practice you finish with 10 or more balls is posted automatically, and every Clubhouse member can see it for two weeks: your name, golfer, area, when it started and ended, how many balls you hit, your good-shot percentage (the share rated 5 or 6), how many stripes (6s) you hit, your best club and its good-shot percentage, the badges you earned during it, and how many cheers it got and from whom. Depending on the panels you choose for the post (Edit post), it can also show each swing's rating and the direction you tagged, in order (the shot map and "Every swing"). Any Clubhouse member can cheer it, which may send you a notification. You can block anyone from their post, crew or board entry; you and they then no longer see each other. Your name is the one in your profile; you can change it any time. Your notes, focus, other tags and round scores are never shared.

Range photos (optional): you can add one photo to a practice's post (Edit post). The app shrinks it on your phone and uploads it to our server (Supabase, Tokyo), where it is kept privately. Before anyone else can see it, it is sent to OpenAI's moderation service, which checks it for content that isn't allowed (for example nudity or violence) and returns only a yes/no result; a photo that fails is deleted. Once it passes, every Clubhouse member can see it on your post for as long as the post shows (not after you delete the practice or your account). Please don't add photos of other people without their permission. You can remove the photo any time in Edit post, which deletes it from our server; deleting your account deletes all your photos.

Notifications: if you allow them, we send cheer and nudge notifications through Expo's push notification service (650 Industries, Inc.), which receives your push token and the notification text.

Deleting: deleting your account (Settings → Delete account) removes your Clubhouse profile, crew membership, push token, the cheers and nudges you sent or received, and the people you blocked, along with your photos and everything else. Reports you made are kept for safety review without your name.

4. Anonymous usage analytics

To understand which features are used and to find problems, the app sends usage events to our analytics provider, PostHog. These events include:

  • Actions in the app, such as starting or finishing a session, logging a swing (with the club and rating), adding a tag or changing a rating, how many swings a session had, and earning a badge.
  • Technical details: device model, operating system version, the version of the app and its analytics library, and your phone's language and time zone settings.
  • A random identifier created by the app. It is not your Apple ID or any advertising identifier, and it resets if you delete and reinstall the app.
  • If the app crashes or hits an error, technical information about the error.

Your internet connection's IP address is necessarily sent with each request, and PostHog may use it to estimate an approximate country or city. We do not combine analytics with any information that identifies you, and we do not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

If the app crashes, it also sends a crash report to our crash reporting provider, Sentry: what went wrong and where in the app's code, plus the device model, operating system version and app version. Crash reports contain no identifier for you, no IP-based location and none of your practice data.

You can turn this off at any time in the app under Settings → "Share anonymous usage data". When it is off, the app sends no analytics and no crash reports.

5. How we use information

  • To run the app's features, including backing up and syncing your practice log and running the Clubhouse if you use them.
  • To understand usage and improve Fairway.
  • To find and fix bugs and crashes.
  • To show how many Fairway golfers have earned each badge. Only rounded percentages across all golfers are published (for example "12% of golfers"); nothing about any one person. The app downloads these percentages from our website at most once a day, which sends only the request itself.

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

6. Service providers and international transfer

Analytics are processed by PostHog, Inc. on servers in the United States. PostHog processes the data on our behalf under its own terms and security measures. See posthog.com/privacy. Crash reports are processed by Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry) on servers in the United States, on the same basis; see sentry.io/privacy. Range photos you add to a post are checked by OpenAI, L.L.C. (United States) through its moderation service, on the same basis; see openai.com/policies/privacy-policy. Because the United States does not have a comprehensive federal privacy law equivalent to Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information, we tell you here that this information is transferred to the United States.

Account and synced data are stored by Supabase Inc. in Tokyo, Japan (section 2). Push notifications go through Expo (650 Industries, Inc., United States; see expo.dev/privacy), which receives only your push token and the notification text.

If you use Fairway outside Japan (for example in the United States), the information you sync is transferred to and stored in Japan, where it is protected under Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information.

7. Keeping, protecting and deleting data

We keep analytics data only as long as it is useful for the purposes above. You can delete everything on your device by deleting the app. Analytics are not linked to your identity, so we usually cannot find a particular person's events, but you can email us and we will help as far as we can, including deleting data where it can be identified.

To protect information, we send all data only over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and our server provider encrypts stored data, photos and backups. On our server, database rules make sure each account can only read and change its own data; what other golfers see comes only through the Clubhouse features described in section 3. Range photos are kept privately and shown to others only after they pass the check. We limit access to the server and the analytics service to the people who work on Fairway, protect that access with strong passwords and two-factor authentication, keep daily backups of the server for 7 days, and choose providers that maintain their own security programs.

8. Children

Fairway is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Future features

We plan to add in-app purchases. Purchases are handled by Apple, and we never receive your payment details. We will update this policy before they go live.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the effective date. For significant changes, we will also let you know in the app.

3. クラブハウス

クラブハウスでは、少人数のグループ（クルー）で仲間と練習を応援し合い、地元チームの一員として地元ボードに参加できます。Fairwayのアカウントがあれば、誰でもボードを見られます。練習する地域を選ぶと（練習のあとにたずねるほか、クラブハウスのタブからいつでも選べます）、その地域のチームに参加します。クラブハウスだけを退会する方法はなく、アカウントを削除すると外れます（下記「削除」を参照）。

参加時に保存する情報：練習する地域（例：東京の区、都道府県、アメリカの州、国。住所や座標は保存しません）、ゴルファーのキャラクター（プロフィールのもの。選ぶまではアプリがランダムに選びます）、タイムゾーン、所属するクルー、送受信した拍手と「行こうよ」、ブロックした相手、送信した報告、プッシュ通知用のトークン。

位置情報：参加時に、現在地から練習する地域を提案できます。現在地を使うボタンをタップしたときだけ許可を求め（標準ではおおよその位置）、1回だけ位置情報を読み取って地域に変換します（地名の検索にはiPhoneがAppleの位置情報サービスを使います）。座標は当社に送信されず、保存されるのは確認した地域だけです。本アプリはモーションデータを使用しません。

ほかの人に見える情報：クルーのメンバーには、名前、ゴルファー、今週の各日の練習日と球数、拍手が見えます。地元ボードでは、練習日と球数が地域の合計として全員に表示され、同じボードのクラブハウス参加者には、トップゴルファーの一覧にあなたの名前、ゴルファー、地域、練習日、球数が表示されることがあります（ブロックした相手には表示されません）。クラブハウスの「みんな」（フィード）には、10球以上の練習を終えると自動で投稿され、2週間、クラブハウスのすべての参加者に表示されます。表示されるのは、名前、ゴルファー、地域、練習の開始・終了時刻、球数、ナイス率（5か6と評価した割合）、会心（6）の数、ベストクラブとそのナイス率、練習中に獲得したバッジ、拍手の数と拍手した人です。投稿に選んだ内容（「投稿を編集」）によっては、1球ごとの評価と記録した方向も打った順に表示されます（ショットマップと「全ショット」）。クラブハウスの参加者は誰でも拍手でき、その際に通知が届くことがあります。投稿、クルー、ボードからどの人もブロックでき、ブロックするとお互いに表示されなくなります。名前はプロフィールの名前で、いつでも変更できます。メモ、目標、その他のタグ、ラウンドスコアは共有されません。

練習場の写真（任意）：練習の投稿に写真を1枚追加できます（「投稿を編集」）。写真はスマホで縮小してから当社のサーバー（Supabase、東京）にアップロードし、非公開で保存します。ほかの人に表示する前に、OpenAIのモデレーションサービスに送り、ヌードや暴力など掲載できない内容がないかを確認します（結果は可否のみ）。確認を通らなかった写真は削除します。確認を通った写真は、投稿が表示されている間、クラブハウスのすべての参加者に表示されます（その練習やアカウントを削除すると表示されなくなります）。ほかの人が写った写真は、その人の許可なく追加しないでください。写真は「投稿を編集」からいつでも削除でき、サーバーからも削除されます。アカウントを削除すると、すべての写真が削除されます。

通知：許可した場合、拍手と「行こうよ」の通知をExpoのプッシュ通知サービス（650 Industries, Inc.）経由で送ります。同サービスはプッシュトークンと通知の文面を受け取ります。

削除：アカウントを削除すると（「設定」→「アカウントを削除」）、写真などすべてのデータとあわせて、クラブハウスのプロフィール、クルーへの所属、プッシュトークン、送受信した拍手と「行こうよ」、ブロックした相手を削除します。あなたが送った報告は、安全確認のため名前を外して保管します。

4. 匿名の利用状況データ

機能の利用状況を把握し、不具合を見つけるため、本アプリは利用状況のデータを分析サービス「PostHog」に送信します。送信する内容は次のとおりです。

  • アプリ内の操作（セッションの開始・終了、スイングの記録とそのクラブ・評価、タグの追加、評価の修正、セッションのスイング数、バッジの獲得など）
  • 技術的な情報（端末の機種、OSのバージョン、アプリおよび分析ライブラリのバージョン、端末の言語・タイムゾーンの設定）
  • 本アプリが生成するランダムな識別子。Apple IDや広告識別子ではなく、アプリを再インストールするとリセットされます。
  • アプリがクラッシュした場合やエラーが起きた場合の、エラーに関する技術情報

通信の仕組み上、IPアドレスが送信され、PostHogがおおよその国・都市の推定に利用することがあります。当社はこれらのデータを、個人を特定できる情報と結び付けません。また、他社のアプリやウェブサイトをまたいだ追跡には使いません。

アプリがクラッシュした場合は、クラッシュレポートをクラッシュ報告サービス「Sentry」にも送信します。内容は、何が起きたかとアプリのコード上の発生箇所、端末の機種、OSのバージョン、アプリのバージョンです。クラッシュレポートには、利用者を識別する情報、IPアドレスから推定した位置情報、練習記録は含まれません。

この送信はいつでも停止できます。アプリの「設定」→「匿名の利用データを送信」をオフにすると、利用状況データもクラッシュレポートも一切送信されません。

5. 利用目的

  • 本アプリの機能（ご利用の場合は練習記録のバックアップ・同期やクラブハウスを含む）を提供するため
  • 利用状況を把握し、本アプリを改善するため
